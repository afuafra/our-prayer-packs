Our Prayer

Privacy Policy · Last updated 21 November 2026

In short: Our Prayer has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no server that collects your data. Your location and settings stay on your phone. We never see them, sell them or share them.

What the app uses and why

  • Location (while using the app). To work out prayer times and the Qibla direction for where you are, and to show nearby mosques. This is calculated on your phone. You can also choose a place by name instead.
  • Location in the background (optional, off by default). Only if you turn on the mosque-arrival feature: the app notices when you arrive at one of the mosques you have added under "My mosques", around prayer time, so it can remind you to silence your phone (or silence it for you, if you allow that). This check happens on your phone and your location is not sent anywhere.
  • Notifications and alarms. To play the adhan or a reminder at prayer and jamaat times. These are scheduled on your phone.
  • Do Not Disturb access (optional). Only if you turn on automatic silencing, so the app can switch your phone to silent at prayer time and back afterwards.

What is stored, and where

Your saved places, chosen mosques, settings and downloaded mosque lists are stored only on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes them. Nothing is uploaded to us.

Third-party services

The app itself sends no personal data to us. Two features contact other services, which receive the ordinary technical information any internet request includes (such as your IP address and device type):

  • Google Maps shows the map on the Mosques tab. Google receives the map area being viewed. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the mosque lists you can download for each country. GitHub receives the download request. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Opening walking directions to a mosque hands off to your maps app (Google Maps or Apple Maps), which follows its own privacy policy.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your choices

You can turn location, notifications and Do Not Disturb access on or off at any time in your phone's settings. The app keeps working without location if you choose a place by name.

Changes
